## What it means (our reading, an estimate)

- Ratios above 50 % do not come from random portal traffic; the games with the most plays sit
  at about 5 %. They come from a community that sees itself in the joke (developers for
  "Deploy on Friday") and shares the link.
- Every top thumbnail shows a character, a face or a joke. None shows abstract shapes.
- So the leaderboard rewards **identity + a share object + a thumbnail that tells the joke**,
  not mechanics.

## Playbook

1. **Pick the community first,** then the joke: developers, office workers, students, gamers,
   parents of pets. The joke must be original (no memes or third-party characters; YouTube
   Playables forbids IP and our gameplay is text-free), so it lives in the character and the
   situation.
2. **Title can carry the joke** on store pages; gameplay stays text-free for YouTube.
3. **Thumbnail:** one character, one action, the accent colour, readable at 128 px, no small
   text. Generate the covers from real gameplay frames with the project's thumbnail script once
   the art exists.
4. **Share object** in the first session (result card, personal image, replay).
5. **Launch post** in the community the joke is for, then Playgama's free traffic run.
6. **Watch the like ratio** as the first health metric next to plays and average play time.
