# Idea set — the factory's first offers (2026-10-20, `brainstorming-game-ideas` + `pebblit-game-dna` + `pebblit-retention` + `pebblit-game-craft`)

Prompt: Hasan ran `/game-trend-skills:brainstorming-game-ideas` right after the Game Factory plan
(`reports/2026-10-20-game-factory-plan.md`) and the two new house skills. This set is what the
factory's PM would put on the table on its first night: three different offers (A closest, B
creative, C our strength), each built from the DNA of games at the top of today's lists and each
checked for originality and for minutes. Carried forward from `reports/2026-10-19-ideas-next-game.md`:
Munch Pixels (A there) is in build, so its family (colour units into containers with waiting
slots) is now our catalog and is avoided here; Cat Exit (B there) returns below in a stronger,
more original form.

## Assumptions

From `game-trend-profile.md` and the factory decisions of 2026-10-20: solo (Hasan) + Claude Code,
Phaser 3 + TypeScript + Vite, budget 0, **no paid boost for any game**, Playgama first and YouTube
Playables later (text-free, 13+, no third-party IP, no off-platform links, portrait). Factory
filters on top of the rubric: a demo buildable in one session and a finish in one day; **Hold ≥ 4**
(`pebblit-retention`), Face and Feel ≥ 3 (`pebblit-game-craft`); **turn-based or without time
pressure**, because the AI gamer plays through screenshots and cannot judge a reflex game;
originality distance ≥ 6 against every parent and no verb-plus-board match with our own games
(`pebblit-game-dna`).

## Generation and cut

Fourteen candidates generated with the operators of `pebblit-game-dna`. Cut:
- **Bolt Bots** (screw sort with robot faces): the colour-units-into-containers family is Munch
  Pixels' family, and the distance to Screwdom is 4 without a deeper rule change.
- **Block puzzle with a monster**, **haunted bus jam**: saturated (block puzzles hold 5 of the
  hypercasual top 10; bus jam was peaking on 2026-10-19). Competition 1.
- **Runner with gates** (Tall Man Run, Build a Queen), **Snake 2048 crossover**, **Shift Shapes**
  runner: real-time, so the AI gamer cannot judge them, and crowded.
- **Ragdoll stress punch** (the Annoying Uncle trend): violence at a person crosses our 13+
  cartoon line, Hold 2.
- **Pop & Sort** (bubble wrap releases marbles into jars): Face 2, Hold 3.
- **Shape Shift Commute** (turn-based form switching): Feel and Virality 3, fun unproven.
- **Pocket Bakery** (merge tycoon with impatient cat customers): economy balancing beyond a day's
  build, crowded merge niche; kept as a later factory candidate.
- **Jigsaw-solitaire reveal**: the payoff overlaps Munch Pixels' reveal.
- **Pumpkin carving relax**: Hold 2, seasonal only.

Scored with `evaluating-game-ideas/scoring-rubric.md`, plus Face and Feel (`pebblit-game-craft`,
each ≥ 3), Hold (`pebblit-retention`, ≥ 4) and the lowest originality distance to any parent
(`pebblit-game-dna`, ≥ 6 of 12).

### Idea: Cat Pile (A, closest) — working title, name to check

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Core loop** | Tap a cat whose way out is clear → it dashes off the furniture and the cats above it tumble down a level, some now facing a free way → empty the pile within the move budget; a blocked tap makes the cat bump and hiss and costs one move, never the run. |
| **Why now** | Arrow Puzzle (Easybrain) #9 global downloads in Sep 2026 and on Playgama's trending list (GamingonPhone from AppMagic, Sep 2026; playgama.com, Oct 2026); cats sell (Meowdoku #7, same source). |
| **Lifecycle stage** | Rising; window 2–4 months (estimate). |
| **Twist** | A muted 3-second clip: one tap, a cat bolts, three cats above it plop down one by one, one lands facing the door and bolts too: a chain exit. |
| **Keep vs change** | Keep: the path-clear tap-away rule, no timer, hints. Change: a pile with gravity instead of a flat grid (chains and planning), cats with moods, no lives (a move budget with instant retry), a daily pile. |
| **Automation lever** | Reverse construction with a gravity simulation guarantees a solution; the solver counts dead ends and chain moves to grade difficulty; endless generated piles after 30 tuned ones. |
| **Social hook** | Chain-exit clips; a daily pile card (moves used), text-free. |
| **Monetization** | Rewarded hint and +3 moves; interstitials between levels behind the gate (≥ 3 levels, ≥ 120 s); meta: a cat album. |
| **MVP scope** | Grid with gravity, the rule, generator and solver, the chain cascade feel, 30 tuned + generated piles, a daily pile; one factory day (estimate); Playgama, Playables-ready. |
| **Not in MVP** | Obstacles beyond three types, costumes, leaderboards. |
| **Hold sketch** | Levels 20–40 s with auto-advance; a new element every 6 levels (a sleepy cat that needs two taps, a kitten that follows its mother, a yarn ball that rolls, a cardboard tunnel, a dog that blocks one side); a chapter every 15 (sofa, bed, bookshelf, rooftop); the album bar never more than 3 levels from the next cat; daily pile with a forgiving streak. Hold 4. |
| **Lineage and distance** | Parent Arrow Puzzle. Kept: the tap-away verb and the path-clear rule. Changed: board (a pile with gravity), face, theme, art; no lives. Distance to Arrow Puzzle 7 (board 3, face 2, theme 1, art 1); to Meowdoku 9. |
| **Kill switch** | Playgama sandbox: 100 plays and 5:00 average (dashboard, 2026-10-18); conversion to play against 65 % (Poki target via Defold's guide); like ratio against the portfolio median. Thresholds are kept live by `tracking-game-metrics`. |
| **Risks** | (1) A crowded niche → the gravity chain and the cats must show in the first second of the clip. (2) A second cat logic game on the market → an art style unlike Meowdoku's. |
| **Scores** | 5 · 5 · 4 · 3 · 2 · 4 · 5 = 28/35; Face 4, Feel 4, Hold 4 |

### Idea: Shy Ghosts (C, our strength) — working title, name to check

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Core loop** | Tap cells to place one ghost in every haunted room so that no ghost can see another along a row or column unless a wall stands between them → a ghost that sees another shivers and turns pale at once → when every ghost is calm the house lights up room by room; no fail, a personal-best clock. |
| **Why now** | Logic dailies keep arriving on Playables (Glowdoku and Zendoku, 6–7 Oct 2026; TubeGames, Oct 2026); puzzles are Playables' strongest category (Mediacube, Sep 2026); Meowdoku #7 global downloads (GamingonPhone from AppMagic, Sep 2026). Halloween on 31 Oct gives the ghosts a social moment now; the theme stays evergreen. |
| **Lifecycle stage** | Rising for logic dailies; a long window for an evergreen genre (estimate). |
| **Twist** | A muted 3-second clip: the last ghost drops in, every pale ghost sighs and glows, they float up together and the windows light one after another to a rising chord. |
| **Keep vs change** | Keep: the daily-logic habit (one puzzle a day, streak, personal best) and pure deduction with a unique solution. Change: a new rule set (rooms plus line of sight blocked by walls) instead of sudoku or queens; ghosts whose shiver teaches the rule without text; generated puzzles. |
| **Automation lever** | Generator + uniqueness solver (backtracking with constraint propagation); difficulty from the deduction steps needed; endless puzzles and a daily seed. |
| **Social hook** | A text-free daily result card (ghost icons and the time); Shorts of the house lighting up, ghost-themed for Halloween. |
| **Monetization** | Rewarded hint (reveals one ghost); interstitials between puzzles behind the gate; meta: ghost costumes by chapter. |
| **MVP scope** | Grid, rules, live shiver feedback, a mark tool for empty cells, generator and solver, a ramp from 4×4 to 9×9, a daily puzzle, 30 picked + generated; one factory day (estimate). |
| **Not in MVP** | Ghost types beyond two, leaderboards. |
| **Hold sketch** | Puzzles 45–120 s, rising; a new element every 8 levels (a lamp that blocks sight, a mirror that bends it, twin ghosts that must see each other, a closet room); a chapter every 15 (attic, library, cellar, greenhouse); costumes as meta; the daily with a forgiving streak. Hold 5. |
| **Lineage and distance** | Parents: queens-style region logic and Meowdoku/Glowdoku (the daily habit) × a line-of-sight rule × our generator with a uniqueness solver. Changed: board (walls inside the grid), constraint (sight lines), face, theme, art. Distance to a queens-style puzzle 9 and to Meowdoku 9. |
| **Kill switch** | Same sandbox and conversion targets as A; for a logic daily, also the share of players who come back for the next day's puzzle (`tracking-game-metrics`). |
| **Risks** | (1) Feel is quieter than in action puzzles → the weight goes on the shiver, the calm and the lights-on finale (Feel spec). (2) A new rule must teach itself without text → three tiny first puzzles with one obvious placement each; the shiver shows the rule. |
| **Scores** | 5 · 5 · 4 · 3 · 3 · 4 · 5 = 29/35; Face 4, Feel 3, Hold 5 |

## Recommendation

Build **C · Shy Ghosts** first. It has the highest total and the best Hold of the three: logic
puzzles with a daily are the surest way past the 5:00 gate that unlocks review, the catalog and
ads, and that gate is the binding constraint without paid traffic. It plays to our strongest
skill (generators with a uniqueness solver), it is text-free by nature, it fits Playables'
strongest category, it is nothing like Munch Pixels or Peek & Find, and its ghosts give us a
Halloween clip now and a theme that lasts. Its weak spot is Feel, so its brief puts the shiver,
the calm and the lights-on finale first. **B · Raccoon Heist** is the best face and clip of the
set and the next bet; **A · Cat Pile** is the quickest build, in the most crowded niche.
