## 0. TL;DR

- **The September download chart is old giants plus two puzzles:** Roblox, Free Fire, Block
  Blast!, Subway Surfers and Bus Fever Party! (Sensor Tower, Sep 2026); Bus Fever Party jumped 141
  places to about 13 M installs (mobilegamer.biz from AppMagic, Sep 2026). → Jam puzzles are
  peaking; we take Bus Fever's cascade (we already did in `pebblit-game-craft`), not its genre.
- **Puzzle money sits in sort, block and screw** (about 600 M USD IAP in H1 2026, sort revenue
  +229 % year on year), **but block had 2,000+ launches in H1 (+89 %) and screw belongs to one
  title** (GameDev Reports from AppMagic, H1 2026; Naavik, 2025). → Avoid block and screw; sort
  only with a twist a muted clip shows.
- **Pixel and conveyor colour puzzles are the hot family, and it is getting contested:** Pixel
  Flow made 198.4 M USD in its first year and peaked in April (PocketGamer.biz from AppMagic,
  2026); Food Hunt sued Colony Flow! over the "ants move pixel blocks" game in July (baijing.cn,
  2026). → Munch Pixels rides this wave on the web: ship it in about two weeks and keep its
  critters, rules and look clearly its own.
- **Logic dailies with a face are rising:** Meowdoku reached 11.5 M installs in September, its
  best month (mobilegamer.biz from AppMagic, Sep 2026), and led Japan's downloads for a second
  month (Sensor Tower, Sep 2026). → Shy Ghosts is on time; its brief is the next build input.
- **YouTube Playables is filling fast:** 788 titles on 1 July, 1,477 in early September
  (Mediacube, Sep 2026), 1,856 by 18 October (TubeGames, Oct 2026); draw-and-escape puzzles lead
  new-game plays (Mediacube, Sep 2026). → The catalogue more than doubled in about 3.5 months;
  enter through Playgama with a proven game, and keep the draw-and-escape idea for the factory.
- **Every rising puzzle draws the same complaints, too many ads and arbitrary walls** (Bus Fever
  Party, Arrows clones, Meowdoku; store and tracker summaries, Sep–Oct 2026). → Our ad gate and
  validated difficulty are the cheapest differentiation we have; never trade them for ad volume.
- **Türkiye is a puzzle exporter, not a market for us:** Turkish developers earned about 2.7–2.8
  B USD in 2025, 97 % of it from puzzles, while players in Türkiye spent 347 M USD (AppMagic
  Türkiye 2026 via Mobidictum and PocketGamer.biz). → Sell globally in English; the local
  questions are payouts and tax, not players.

### 1.4 Monetization trend

- Top mobile puzzles earn through IAP plus ads (Pixel Flow's 198.4 M USD is IAP only;
  PocketGamer.biz, 2026). Our channels are ads-only: YouTube Playables has no IAP and plans tests
  in 2027 (shattered.io, Sep 2026; PocketGamer.biz).
- No 2026 eCPM table by country was found. The newest: US rewarded 16.49–19.63 USD and
  interstitial 14.08–14.32 USD (Appodeal, Q4 2024; dated); Türkiye not listed.
- Our own web rate: ≈ 1.4 USD per 1,000 plays, ≈ 1.5 USD per 1,000 ads (**estimate**: 0.07 USD a
  day ÷ ≈ 50 plays a day ÷ 0.91 ads per play; Peek & Find day 2, mixed traffic,
  `metrics/peek-and-find.md`). Web money comes from minutes and volume, not from ad pressure.
- Complaints in 1–3 star reviews across the risers: too many or unskippable ads (Bus Fever
  Party, Arrow Puzzle – Tap Away, Arrows GO!, Meowdoku), late levels that feel arbitrary or
  paywalled (Bus Fever Party past level 300), unsolvable levels (Meowdoku). Store and tracker
  summaries, Sep–Oct 2026.

## 5. Opportunities

1. **Pixel-eating colour puzzle on the web (Munch Pixels).** Window: now to about February 2027
   (**estimate**), while mobile leaders cool off after April's peak and the web has no clear
   leader. We can win by speed (Epics 4–6 left), a solver that proves every level (the answer
   to Bus Fever's "arbitrary" reviews), a face in the reveal and Bridge's reach. Condition: an
   originality check against Food Hunt, Colony Flow! and Pixel Flow before the art goes in,
   because the leaders are suing each other.
2. **Logic daily with a character (Shy Ghosts).** Window: long, an evergreen genre now rising
   (Meowdoku). We win with uniqueness-proved puzzles, a forgiving streak and gated ads, the three
   things Meowdoku's reviews ask for (`reports/2026-10-20-eval-shy-ghosts.md`).
3. **Draw-and-escape for Playables (the factory's Raccoon Heist idea).** Window: while Playables
   grows; the category leads new-game plays (Mediacube, Sep 2026). We win with generated levels
   and fast iteration, but the catalogue doubles every few months, so this needs the factory's
   speed, not a hand build.

## 6. Avoid (for now)

- **Block puzzles:** saturated; Block Blast holds #1 downloads and H1 2026 saw 2,000+ new block
  games (+89 %).
- **Screw puzzles:** one title owns the subgenre and downloads fell 12 % in 2025.
- **Anything that looks like Food Hunt or Colony Flow!** (ants carrying pixel blocks): the two
  are in a copyright lawsuit since July 2026; a lookalike invites a takedown.
- **Punching a person for laughs** (the Annoying Uncle trend on Playables): it crosses our 13+
  content rule however well it plays.
