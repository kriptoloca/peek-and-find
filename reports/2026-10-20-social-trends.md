# Social trends → games (2026-10-20, `scouting-social-trends`)

Scope: what spreads on TikTok, Shorts, Reels and Roblox in October 2026, cut down to verbs and run
through the five gates for Pebblit's text-free, 13+, IP-free web games (`game-trend-profile.md`).
Collected with live search on 2026-10-20. TikTok Creative Center, Know Your Meme and the trend
trackers do not open from the build machine, so every line is a search summary (page not opened);
most roundups are marketing blogs with daily-changing samples. No October 2026 Meme of the Month,
AI-filter trend or Türkiye-specific trend was found.

## Meme rule applied

The steal wave runs on characters other people own (brainrot figures) and the blind-box wave on
Labubu. We take the structure and make our own meme-able cast: the sock-stealing raccoon, the shy
ghosts (one brave one in sunglasses), the munchers. Each new character is a new clip.

## Single best bet

**The "steal" verb, with our own raccoon (Raccoon Heist).** It is the only trend that passes all
five gates; it has carried hits for a year on Roblox (Steal a Brainrot, then Steal an Egg); it
maps onto draw-and-escape, the category that leads new-game plays on YouTube Playables
(Mediacube, Sep 2026, in `reports/2026-10-20-insight-report.md`); and it comes with a ready video
format ("getting robbed"). It keeps its place after Munch Pixels and Shy Ghosts in the queue, as
the next new game or the factory's first demo; the window is long enough for that.

**Fastest win this week:** the glass-candy crunch in Munch Pixels' Epic 4 (feel and art): it
rides the ASMR wave at no extra build.
