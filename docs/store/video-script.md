# 15-second gameplay video (no voice, no text)

Capture from the production build at 1080×1920 (portrait) and once more at 1920×1080; 30 fps;
game audio only (SFX on, music on). Use the real art pack. Cuts are hard cuts, no transitions.

| Time | Shot | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0–0.6 s | Title | PLAY tapped; the finger (cursor hidden) leaves the frame. |
| 0.6–3.5 s | Level 2, kitchen, Find | Scene pops in; two targets found in two taps, sparkle and sound each time; stars overlay for 0.4 s. |
| 3.5–7.0 s | Level 6, Odd One Out (the first Odd of the rhythm) | Grid of near-identical items; a 1 s look, then the odd one tapped; confetti. |
| 7.0–11.5 s | Level 11, beach, Spot 5 (the first Spot of the rhythm) | Stacked images; three differences tapped in a row, the fourth found after a short pause, the fifth found; win. |
| 11.5–14.0 s | Level 25, city, big scene | Dense scene, two quick finds, one wrong tap (shake, heart lost) to show the stakes, one more find. |
| 14.0–15.0 s | Freeze on the next scene popping in | Hold the last frame; no end card, no text. |

Rules: no device frame, no hands, no captions, no logos beyond the in-game wordmark on the
Title; the first second must already show the game (platform previews start muted and loop).
